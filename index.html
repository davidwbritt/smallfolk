How Smallfolk works

The two-minute version, with no assumed knowledge. Prefer to watch it happen? Read a whole worked session — every tool, one Friday night, one real seed.

The one-sentence version: every click of New village invents a complete little village — its people, their secrets, and one mystery — and the web address IS the village: copy the URL, send it to a friend, and they see exactly the same place.

The one-rule version: everything in black ink is public — read it aloud, show it to anyone. Everything in red ink is the secret truth, meant for the person running the game. Player view (top bar) hides all the red at once.

If you're running a game tonight

  1. Click New village (or the ‹ › arrows) until you find one you like.
  2. Read the red ink — the secrets, the rumor answers, and “The Trouble” box at the bottom. That's the plot, pre-loaded.
  3. Take it with you: Print / PDF for a handout, or Copy markdown to paste into your notes app.
  4. At the table, when players ask around town: roll a d8 on the rumor table and read what they hear. You already know which rumors are lies.
  5. Sharing your screen? Hit Player view first. The red disappears.

If you're playing solo

  1. Turn Player view ON — so the mystery stays hidden from you too.
  2. Open the Journal (top bar) and press ⚑ Begin. It writes your arrival scene for you.
  3. Play by asking yes/no questions out loud — “Does the innkeeper trust me?” — then click likely?, even?, or unlikely? in the dice tray. The oracle answers; you write what it means.
  4. Meeting someone new? Click reaction?. Stuck or bored? Click ✶ Something happens in the journal, or ✶ spark in the tray.
  5. Everything you roll logs itself into the journal while it's open. When the session ends, Export copies the whole story as text — post it, keep it, or burn it with Clear.
  6. When you're ready to know the truth, switch off Player view and read the red.
